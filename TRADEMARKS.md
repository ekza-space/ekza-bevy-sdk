# Ekza names and branding

We use **Ekza**, **EKZA** and the Ekza logos to identify the official SDK, the Ekza services and their releases. The `MIT OR Apache-2.0` license of this crate covers code, not names or logos: it does not grant trademark rights. This policy does not assert that any name is registered or that rights exist in every country; any enforceable rights remain subject to applicable law.

You may truthfully say that a game or tool “uses the Ekza SDK”, “supports the Ekza protocol” or is “compatible with Ekza avatars” when that is accurate. Descriptive attribution does not need approval.

A fork or independently operated service needs its own product/service name and logo. Do not present it as official Ekza software, an Ekza registry or an Ekza-approved game, and do not imply endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation that has not been granted. Keep the copyright and license notices even when you replace the visible branding.

Labels such as “approved for Ekza” or “Ekza verified” refer to acceptance by the official registry; use them only for assets or games that actually received that acceptance.

Requests for endorsement or logo use beyond truthful reference go to the maintainers listed on the [project repository](https://github.com/ekza-space/ekza-bevy-sdk).

These rules concern origin and confusion about affiliation. They do not prohibit commercial forks, criticism, comparison, nominative/fair use or other uses permitted by law, and they do not amend the software license. Avatars and other content delivered through an Ekza registry remain subject to their own rights holders' terms.
